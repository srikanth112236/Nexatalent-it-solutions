# Company Context

## 1. Company

NexaTalent is a modern recruitment and talent solutions company supported by a technology-enabled recruitment operating platform.

It connects employers, hiring managers, recruiters, internal employees, candidates, jobs, talent pools, ATS workflows, interviews, CRM, tasks, targets, documents, notifications and reporting.

NexaTalent must feel like a **credible recruitment company powered by technology**, not a generic SaaS product pretending to be a recruitment business.

## 3. Recruitment lifecycle

`Requirement → Qualification → Job → Sourcing → Screening → ATS → Shortlist → Employer Submission → Review → Interview → Feedback → Offer → Joining`

## 4. Roles

### Super Admin

Platform owner/operator. Full administrative capability subject to explicit permissions and audit controls.

### Employee

Internal NexaTalent user. Access is permission-driven and can represent operations, sales, management or recruitment support.

### Recruiter

Internal recruitment specialist working with jobs, candidates, talent pool, ATS, interviews and employer coordination.

### Employer

External organization user. Must only access that organization's authorized requirements, jobs, candidates, interviews, documents and reports.

### Candidate

External talent user. Must only access their own profile, resume, applications, interviews, documents, notifications and settings.

## 5. Brand personality

Intelligent. Human. Modern. Reliable. Precise. Professional. Technology-enabled. Confident. Accessible.

Avoid generic stock-recruitment language, fake metrics, fake client logos, exaggerated promises and unnecessary visual noise.

## 6. Core message

> NexaTalent connects people, businesses and opportunity through a structured, technology-enabled recruitment experience.
