# Content Strategy

## Positioning

Do not position NexaTalent merely as “a recruitment agency”. Position it as a recruitment and talent solutions company combining human expertise, structured processes and technology-enabled hiring workflows.

## Audiences

1. Employers and hiring leaders
2. Candidates and professionals
3. Recruitment/internal operations teams

## Content pillars

- Hiring Solutions
- Industry Expertise
- Talent
- Recruitment Operations
- Employer Intelligence
- Career Intelligence
- Proof

## Homepage narrative

`Problem → Promise → Audience → Solution → Process → Proof → Conversion`

## Employer page narrative

`Need → Approach → Process → Visibility → Outcome`

## Candidate narrative

Helpful, transparent, respectful and action-oriented. Never guarantee placement or employment.

## Service page template

Hero → Business Problem → Who It Is For → Solution → Process → Capabilities → Platform → Proof → FAQ → CTA

## Industry page template

Hero → Hiring Context → Challenges → Roles/Skills → Approach → Solutions → Proof → Jobs → Insights → CTA

## Job page content

Title, location, work mode, employment type, experience, approved compensation, summary, responsibilities, skills, qualifications, benefits, hiring process, apply, save and related jobs.

## Case study

Challenge → Context → Requirement → Approach → Execution → Recruitment Process → Result → Client Perspective.

## Insights engine

Hiring Guides, Talent Trends, Interview Guides, Salary & Market Insights, Recruitment Playbooks, Career Guides, Industry Hiring Reports and Case Studies.

## SEO clusters

Recruitment services, permanent hiring, contract staffing, executive search, volume hiring, GCC hiring, industry hiring, job SEO, location SEO and recruitment resources.

## Conversion

Employer primary CTA: `Submit Hiring Requirement`.
Employer secondary CTA: `Talk to a Talent Specialist`.
Candidate primary CTA: `Find Jobs`.
Candidate secondary CTA: `Create Profile`.

## Governance

Content lifecycle: Draft → Review → Approved → Published → Updated → Archived.

Never invent metrics, testimonials, client logos or case-study outcomes.
