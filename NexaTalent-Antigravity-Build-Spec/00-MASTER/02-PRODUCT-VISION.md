# Product Vision

## Vision

Make recruitment more structured, visible and connected — from hiring requirement to successful talent engagement.

## North-star problem

Recruitment often becomes fragmented across emails, spreadsheets, job portals, candidate databases, interview coordination, client communication and reporting. NexaTalent provides one connected operating model.

## Product pillars

1. **Employer experience** — clear hiring requests, candidate review, interviews, feedback and reporting.
2. **Candidate experience** — jobs, profile, resume, applications, interviews and notifications.
3. **Recruiter productivity** — sourcing, screening, talent pool, ATS, interviews, tasks and targets.
4. **Client growth** — leads, companies, contacts, opportunities, activities, requirements and pipeline.
5. **Governance** — RBAC, organization scope, auditability, document security and administrative control.

## Experience principles

- Clarity before complexity.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Role-specific workflows.
- Action-oriented dashboards.
- Trust and privacy by design.
- Consistent components across surfaces.
- Mobile responsive, not merely desktop-shrunk.

## Dashboard rule

Every dashboard should answer:

1. What is happening?
2. What needs attention?
3. What changed?
4. What should I do next?

## Long-term extension points

- Agency/partner ecosystem
- AI candidate matching
- AI recruiter copilot
- Talent intelligence
- Enterprise integrations
- Native mobile applications

These must remain extension points and must not complicate the current implementation.
